## Final Demo Script

1. Login with Google.
2. Add five mixed deadline tasks.
3. Open AI Planner and generate an AI plan.
4. Show priority, deadline risk, breakdown, daily plan, and emergency plan.
5. Open Add Task and use voice command text:

```text
Remind me to submit my project report tomorrow at 6 PM. It will take 3 hours and it is very important.
```

6. Click Parse with Gemini and show auto-filled form.
7. Save task and return to dashboard.
8. Connect Google Calendar.
9. Add one task to Google Calendar.
10. Mark one task complete and show dashboard update.
